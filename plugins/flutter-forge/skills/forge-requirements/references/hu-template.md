# Default user story template (hu-v1)

Projects can replace it with `requirements.template` in the profile. Keep the section numbers
and the `### <surface>` convention when adapting it: consumers map sections by number.
Write the story in `requirements.language`; Gherkin keywords stay in English.

Rules
- Section numbers and titles are fixed. A section that does not apply keeps its heading and one
  line: `N/A — <reason>`.
- Unknown values: `[TBD: what is missing]`. Never invent amounts, limits, legal rules or dates.
- Self-contained: rule values live in this file, not in references to other documents.
- IDs: HU-n, EP-n; AC-n, NFR-n, R-n, Q-n unique inside the story (global ref: HU-n/AC-n).
- One behavior per AC, declarative, with observable values. Split the story if Points > 8.
